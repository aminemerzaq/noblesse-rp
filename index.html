CODE OF CONDUCT

Server Rules

Our rules are the foundation of the Noblesse experience. Respect them, or face the consequences.

01

Always Stay In Character

Maintain your character's perspective at all times during active gameplay. OOC (Out Of Character) communication must use designated channels only.

02

Value Your Life

Your character must act as if their life has value. Reckless disregard for your own safety (known as No Value of Life / NVL) is prohibited.

03

No Metagaming

Using information obtained outside the game (Discord, streams, etc.) to influence in-game decisions is a serious violation and will result in bans.

04

No Random Deathmatch

Initiating violence without valid in-character reason (RDM/VDM) is forbidden. Every conflict must have a believable narrative behind it.

05

Respect All Players

Harassment, discrimination, or toxic behavior toward any community member, in or out of character, will result in immediate removal.

06

Follow Staff Decisions

Staff decisions are final during active situations. All disputes must be submitted via the official ticket system after the session concludes.
